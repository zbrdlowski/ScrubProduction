## Your personal board

Open **Kanban** in the top menu, to the left of Attendance, or **My kanban** in the sidebar. Each signed-in user has their own columns and cards. Changes are saved automatically after a move or when you submit a form.

## Choosing and reopening templates

On your first visit, choose **General**, **Design** or **Customer service**. A template supplies starting columns that you can customise afterwards.

1. Click **Templates** at the top right, next to Help, to reopen the initial chooser.
2. Select a template and read the confirmation.
3. **Apply template** replaces the existing columns. All active cards move to the first column of the new template; all completed cards move to the last. The previous distribution of active cards across columns is not retained.
4. All cards, including older history, their content, completion dates and your display limit are preserved. New columns use the currently selected language.

**Back to board** closes the chooser without changes. Simply opening templates or cancelling the confirmation does not change your board. To adjust just one column, use its three-dot menu.

## Adding and editing a card

Click **New card** or **Add card** in a column. Enter a title and optionally add a description, priority, due date, label, customer or reference, and a complete link starting with `https://` or `http://`. Click **Save card**.

Click a card title or its pencil icon to open the details. You can change the column in this form too. An order or project link is a reference only; moving a personal card does not change the source order or project status.

## Moving cards and columns

- Drag a card by its body or title into any column. You can also reorder cards within active columns.
- Move an entire column using the dotted handle beside its name.
- On a touch screen, briefly hold the card or handle before dragging. Scroll the board horizontally to reach more columns.
- To move without dragging, use the card form's column selector, the arrows on cards, or the arrows in column settings.

## Custom columns

**Add column** creates a new column. Its three-dot menu opens the name, colour, order and **Cards in this column are completed** setting. A board supports up to 20 columns.

The completion setting affects every card in the column, including older hidden cards. A column can only be deleted after all its cards have been moved elsewhere. At least one column must remain.

## Completed cards and history

**Done: latest** sets the number of visible cards in each completed column: 1 to 100, with a default of 20. Older cards are kept; click **Load older** to see them.

Completed cards are sorted newest first by completion time, rather than manually. Returning a card to an active column clears its completion; completing it again records a new time. Moving between two completed columns keeps the original completion time.

Active columns load 100 cards at a time. Click **Load more** for the next page. Refreshing or saving a change applies the display limit again.

## Search

Search includes older hidden cards and matches titles, descriptions, labels and references. Dragging is disabled while searching. Clear the search to drag again, or move a card through its details.

## Language and saving

**SK / EN** switches the interface and this guide. Your choice is remembered for your account in this browser. Your own card and column names and text are not automatically translated.

If the connection is interrupted or the board changes in another window, a message appears. Use the circular-arrow **Refresh board** button to load the current state. After a conflict, copy any unsaved text you need, close the form, refresh the board and reopen the card.

Deleting a card requires confirmation and is permanent. To keep finished work, move the card to a completed column instead.
